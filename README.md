# Skypmine Combat Addon

Skypmine Combat Addon is a Minecraft Fabric combat mod featuring Crystal Aura, KillAura, TriggerBot, FastEat, AntiBlind, and E-Chest Farmer, with customizable settings for combat automation and inventory management.

A Minecraft Fabric combat addon featuring six modules designed for different PvP situations:

- **Custom Crystal Aura** – An advanced crystal combat module with three modes:
  - **Selected** – Places and breaks crystals on your selected obsidian blocks. For 100% reliable operation, the wall height must be limited to **2 blocks**. All obsidian blocks inside the trap can be used, but there must be **no obsidian on your side** of the trap.
  - **Looking At** – Places and breaks crystals on the obsidian block you're looking at. Best suited for **Air PvP / regular PvP**.
  - **Any Obsidian** – Works with any valid obsidian blocks. The last two modes are intended for **traps**.

- **Custom E-Chest Farmer** – Automates ender chest farming.
- **Custom KillAura** – Automatically attacks nearby targets.
- **Custom TriggerBot** – Automatically attacks when your crosshair is on a target.
- **Custom AntiBlind** – Prevents blindness effects from obstructing your vision.
- **FastEat** – Speeds up eating.

Built for Minecraft Fabric, with configurable settings for different combat situations.
